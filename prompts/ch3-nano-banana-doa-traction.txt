================================================================================
1) SCHÉMA DOA — Voix → Raspberry → Arduino + MPU5060 → rotation → caméra sur le torse
================================================================================

Professional academic technical diagram, clean flat vector style, white background, soft blue and gray accents, French labels optional but clear English OK.

Title at top: "DOA and corrected rotation — KODA robot"

Flow left to right in 5 numbered steps:
(1) Circular ReSpeaker 4-mic array icon on a small robot silhouette, label "Voice detected — DOA angle"
(2) Raspberry Pi block, label "Target angle calculated"
(3) USB serial arrow toward Arduino Uno + L293D shield, label "ROTATE(angle)"
(4) Arduino block connected to MPU5060 by I2C A4/A5, label "Gyro feedback — corrected stop"
(5) Top-down tracked robot rotating with motors M1 and M3, then front view with camera lens fixed on CHEST/TORSO, label "Camera on body — user oriented"

Important: head/Nextion screen may be separate small note "S3 servo = Nextion face only (does NOT move camera)".
Important: MPU5060 must be shown connected to Arduino, not to Raspberry Pi.

No photorealistic photo, no Fritzing, no 3D render game style. Infographic for engineering PFE report. 16:9, high resolution, minimal text, arrows between steps.

Negative prompt: MPU connected to Raspberry GPIO, wrong camera on head, single wheel car, complex equations, dark background, watermark.

================================================================================
2) TRACTION DIFFÉRENTIELLE — Moteurs M1 et M3
================================================================================

Professional educational diagram, top-down view, white background, vector style for university robotics report.

Title: "Differential drive — motors M1 & M3"

Show a rectangular robot chassis with:
- Left track/wheel driven by motor M1
- Right track/wheel driven by motor M3
- Center of rotation point marked between wheels

Three small inset panels or arrows:
A) Both wheels forward — arrow "Forward"
B) Wheels opposite directions — curved arrow "Turn in place (pivot)"
C) One wheel faster — gentle arc "Turn while moving"

Labels: M1 left, M3 right. Simple tank-tread robot like KODA base.

Style: clean textbook illustration, not photo, not Fritzing wiring. Colors: dark gray tracks, blue motors, black arrows. 16:9, publication quality.

Negative prompt: 4-wheel car, steering wheel, airplane, photorealistic, cluttered text.
